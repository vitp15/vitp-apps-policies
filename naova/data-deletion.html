Delete Your Data

Naova has no account and no server: Everything you log is stored only on your own device. That means you are always in control of your data and can remove it yourself, instantly.

Delete individual entries

Open any attack, check-in, or cycle entry in the app and delete it. The change takes effect immediately on your device.

Delete everything

Uninstall Naova from your device. Because your data lives only in the app's private on-device storage, uninstalling permanently removes all of it — attacks, check-ins, cycle entries, and any saved weather context. This cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Backups you exported

If you previously exported a backup file, it is wherever you chose to save it (for example your own cloud drive or Files). Delete that file from that location to remove that copy.

Anonymous diagnostics

Naova sends only pseudonymous, non-health diagnostic and crash information to help keep the app stable; it is not linked to your identity. Uninstalling the app stops any further collection. If you would like the Service Provider to delete diagnostic data associated with your app instance, email the address below with the subject Data Deletion Request and we will action it, normally within 30 days.
